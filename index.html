Commercial Analyst

Mamikos – Commercial Analyst

As a Commercial Analyst at Mamikos, I analyzed business and transaction data for OKHome to address business problems and generate actionable insights and recommendations. Within one month, I completed approximately five business analyses, delivering dashboards, insights, and strategic recommendations presented to stakeholders across areas including service performance, customer migration, customer journey, customer behavior, user segmentation, repeat cycle, subscription performance, demand trends, and business expansion opportunities. Within my first four working days, I identified an anomaly in the transaction data, which led to an evaluation and improvement of the transaction calculation methodology, increasing the accuracy and reliability of the data used for analysis. One of the recommendations from my first analysis, completed during my first week, was approved and implemented by stakeholders, with initial results showing approximately 20% growth in transactions and revenue compared with the previous month, with further growth still possible. I also analyzed the drivers behind changes in demand, customer characteristics contributing to those changes, and the impact of price increases and long weekends on customer behavior and service performance. The analyses were conducted using SQL (BigQuery), Power BI, Microsoft Excel, and Google Sheets, including DAX, Pivot Tables, Slicers, Charts, VBA, Lookup, and LET. Beyond technical skills, this experience strengthened my problem-solving and independent working abilities, from understanding business needs and defining analytical approaches to developing step-by-step solutions and proactively identifying additional business questions and opportunities that could be addressed through available data.
